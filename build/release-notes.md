## Jarvis 1.10.0 — To-Do planner

- **New To-Do planner** built into Jarvis. Open it with the TO-DO button at the top, the To-Do panel on the right, or say "Hey Jarvis, open my to-do". It opens with an animated circular reveal from the button.
- **Calendar view:** week grid you can drag tasks around on, a month view, and an unscheduled tray.
- **List view:** everything in order of when it's due (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, and so on).
- **Tags:** green Small, yellow Medium, red Important.
- **Voice and chat:** "add math homework tomorrow 4pm important", "what's due today", "mark gym done", "move essay to friday".
- **Reminders:** Jarvis speaks up 10 minutes before timed tasks (toggle this in Settings).
- **Saved on your PC** with 7 days of daily backups. Moving tasks from the web version: press "Copy tasks" there, then paste them into "Bring in tasks".

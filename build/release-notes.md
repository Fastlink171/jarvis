## M.I.K.U 1.22.0: steadier brain

- **Speech recognition now runs on the processor by default**, leaving the graphics card to the AI brain. Sharing the card was making AMD's driver crash with "ROCm error". If you prefer the old way, turn on "Run hearing on the graphics card" in Settings.
- **Automatic recovery:** if the AI engine still crashes, Miku restarts it and tries your request again, instead of showing an error.
